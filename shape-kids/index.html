FAQ

Questions from parents.

Is this adult weight training?

No. Sessions focus on suitable movement skills, technique, and exercises supervised by a child-qualified instructor.

Where do classes take place?

At Shape Training Club. A designated area is reserved exclusively for the kids group during class time.

Can I request personal training?

Yes. One-to-one sessions can be arranged, subject to suitability and availability.

Where are the prices and class times?

Contact our team for the finalized schedule and fees. No time or price is confirmed by this page.
