Frequently asked questions

How do I sign in? Enter your email address, then the one-time code we send you. No password is needed. The code email is in Turkish and English.

I can't see my session balance. Your coach starts your package after payment; once started, your remaining sessions appear on your home screen.

I'm not getting notifications. Allow notifications in your phone's Settings → Notifications → SHAPE Coaching.

My coach's notes are in Turkish. The app's own texts are translated. What your coach writes stays as written; touch and hold the text and choose Translate on your iPhone.

How do I delete my account? In the app, go to Profile → Delete my account. Your account and all your data are permanently deleted.
